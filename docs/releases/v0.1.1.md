# v0.1.1

## Monthly update without the crawl state

- `jwd monthly -dataset <dir>` works like `jwd month`: it loads the
  stations, the homogeneity breaks and the year and month files of the
  recent years from a dataset directory into a temporary state, downloads
  the station list, probes new stations, refetches from the first day of
  the month before last to yesterday and exports. The oldest month file
  goes into its year file; files of other years are not needed and not
  touched.
- It refuses to run when a year file listed in `manifest.json` is missing
  or a month file is older than the years it reads, so that an incomplete
  download cannot lose rows.
- `scripts/jwd-monthly.sh` downloads those files from the Hub, runs it and
  uploads the result (deleting the merged month file);
  `.github/workflows/monthly.yml` runs it in this image on the 2nd of each
  month (about 260 requests, well within a GitHub-hosted job). The daily
  and monthly workflows share a concurrency group.

## Changes

- The monthly run no longer refetches a share of the older station-years
  (`-refresh-cycle-months`, `-refresh-age` and `-out` are removed). Changes
  to past years are left to JMA's announced corrections (planned).
- The dataset card takes the range of years from the stations' coverage
  as well, so a partial state gives the full range.
- `ImportRows` loads several files of a year in one call (a year file and
  its month files).
